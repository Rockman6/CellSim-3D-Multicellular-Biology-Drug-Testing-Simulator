CellSim

A mechanistic simulator of cultured cells under drug, with its errors measured.

Each simulated cell runs a cell-cycle oscillator, the ATM/p53/MDM2 damage response and the intrinsic apoptotic cascade, driven by the drug concentration inside it. Run them well-mixed and a dose-response curve is one simulation; run them on a lattice and a spheroid grows a hypoxic rim and a necrotic core.

Run an experiment in your browser

Open the Lab → Pick cells and a drug, knock out a gene, change the schedule, press Run. The real engine runs on your own machine — nothing to install, nothing uploaded. The first visit downloads about 40 MB and takes a minute or two to start; after that, about half a minute per experiment.

Watch inside one cell → Every molecule the engine tracks, minute by minute, as a cell divides, stops or dies. Look up any drug by name or structure, or design your own and watch what it does.

Run it yourself

The cell simulator needs only NumPy and SciPy:

pip install cellsim

cellsim cell-sim --line A549 --drug cisplatin --hours 72
cellsim dish --line DLD-1 --geometry spheroid --hours 120 \
    --cells 3000 --grid 48 --every 8 --out spheroid.jsonl

Then open spheroid.jsonl with the viewer's “Open .jsonl” button. In a notebook:

from cellsim.api import curve, exposure, washout, spheroid

exposure("A549", "paclitaxel")   # concentration needed for half kill
                                 # at each exposure time

What it is good for, and what it is not

It answers questions within a cell line, where the mechanism carries the weight: whether to pulse a drug or hold it, how long a culture takes to recover after wash-out, whether two drugs antagonise, what a schedule selects for, how deep a drug reaches into an aggregate.

It does not predict which cell line is more sensitive. That was measured to be unreachable from the available markers — eleven of them, fitted optimally across 156–683 cell lines, explain 9–20 % of the difference between lines — so it is stated as out of scope rather than attempted.

How far to trust it

Every fitted quantity is named and counted: one potency constant per drug, one spatial constant for the dish. Everything else is a literature input cited where it is used. Predictions are compared against public measurements, and the misses are published beside the passes — including two results withdrawn during development when re-measuring showed one rested on a single surviving lineage and the other on one random seed.
